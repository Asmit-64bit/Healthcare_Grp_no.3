Complete Blood Count (CBC)

Purpose: To evaluate overall health and detect a wide range of disorders, including anemia and infection.

Sample Required: Blood sample drawn from a vein.

Preparation Instructions: Fasting is required for at least 8 hours prior to the test.

Normal Reference Range: Varies by age and gender (e.g., Male Hemoglobin: 13.8 to 17.2 g/dL).


Report Delivery Time: Within 24 hours.

Important Instructions:

Please inform the technician if you are on any blood-thinning medication.
